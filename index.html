What the estate is like in person

Mornings here start in mist. By the time it lifts, the pickers are already moving through the rows, taking only the bud and the first two leaves — the part that becomes our orthodox tea.

A visit runs two to three hours: a walk through the gardens, a stop at the withering floor, and a tasting of whatever's fresh that week.

Good to know before you come

Bring closed shoes — the garden paths are soft after rain. Groups larger than 15 should book at least three days ahead so we can arrange a second guide.

Our branches

Each branch sits at a slightly different elevation, which changes the leaf — and the visit.

  • Mechinagar Main Garden

    The original estate. Largest tour route, home to our tasting room.

  • Kamal Basti Branch

    Smaller, quieter gardens — best for visitors who want fewer people around.

  • Charpane Branch

    Highest elevation of the three, with a view over the plantation valley.
